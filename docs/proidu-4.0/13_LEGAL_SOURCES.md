# 13. Legal and official source register

This is an engineering source register, not a legal opinion. Every rule must be checked against the official publication and current edition before it is enabled in production.

## Russian admission
- Federal Law 273-FZ, Education in the Russian Federation. Relevant areas include licensing/accreditation, admission, foreign citizens and recognition of foreign education.
- Ministry of Science and Higher Education Order 821 dated 27 November 2024, as amended by Order 905 dated 26 November 2025. It governs admission to bachelor, specialist and master programmes and is stated to remain in force until 1 September 2029.
- University admission rules and campaign documents for each institution and admission year.
- Rosobrnadzor licence register and state accreditation register.
- Minobrnauki open data and official campaign publications.

## Foreign applicants
- 273-FZ article 78: foreign citizens' access to education under international treaties, federal law, government quota and paid contracts.
- Government Resolution 2150 dated 18 December 2020: government quota for foreign citizens.
- Order 821: foreign applicants may be subject to separate entrance exam arrangements; the institution's actual published rules must be captured.
- Recognition of foreign education: 273-FZ article 107 and the applicable recognition register/process.
- Official Education in Russia portal: https://russia-edu.minobrnauki.gov.ru/

A draft government resolution dated 2 September 2026 concerning a quota-admission service was found marked as unsigned. It is a monitoring item only. Do not encode draft clauses as operative rules until official publication and effective date are confirmed.

## Personal data
- Federal Law 152-FZ, Personal Data.
- Article 18(5), amended by Federal Law 23-FZ dated 28 February 2025 and effective 1 July 2025: Russian citizens' personal data collected online must use databases located in Russia for the specified primary operations, subject to statutory exceptions.
- Article 12: cross-border transfer requirements.
- Article 19: security measures.
- Operator obligations, privacy policy, consent, processing contracts and retention must be assessed for the actual legal entity and processing purposes.

## Official data source rules
- Preserve canonical source URL and retrieval timestamp.
- Preserve original document or source snapshot where lawful.
- Attribute public datasets and do not distort source data.
- Do not bypass authentication, anti-bot controls or access restrictions.
- Do not assume that an official portal offers a public API.
- Do not automate submission to Gosuslugi, quota systems or university applicant accounts without documented authorisation.

## Legal rule lifecycle
Each legal rule stores document identifier, article/paragraph, official URL, publication date, effective-from/effective-to, amendment chain, jurisdiction, affected applicant categories, reviewed-by and review date. Draft, consultation, adopted-but-not-effective and effective rules are distinct statuses.
